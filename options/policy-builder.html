Organization palette: policy builder

First time setting this up? Follow the step by step setup guide.

Pick your brand colors here, copy the result, and paste it into the Google Admin console. Nothing is sent anywhere: this page runs entirely in your browser and has no server behind it.

What to publish

On is the usual choice, and it is what most administrators expect when they publish brand colors: your colors become the palette staff start on in every picker, and they cannot be edited. Turn it off to publish them as a starting point staff may personalize instead — useful when teams are trusted to deviate, but it does mean someone can quietly end up off-brand. Either way, palettes staff created themselves are kept, never deleted, and they can still keep their own alongside yours.

Brings in every palette from this browser so you do not retype names and hex codes. Your own palettes are only read, never changed.

Where it goes

  1. Sign in to admin.google.com as a Workspace administrator.
  2. Go to Devices → Chrome → Apps & extensions → Users & browsers.
  3. Select the org unit the staff are in.
  4. Click Custom Color Palette for Google Workspace Docs, Sheets & Slides in the list.
    • A new organization's list is empty, so if it is not there, add it:
      • Click the yellow + button (bottom right).
      • Choose Add from Chrome Web Store.
      • Search for Custom Color Palette.
      • Click it, then click Select.
    • Use the Web Store option, not Add Chrome app or extension by ID. That one is for extensions you host yourself and will ask you for an installation URL.
  5. Scroll to Policy for extensions, select whatever is in the box, and paste the above generated text over it.
  6. Click SAVE, in the upper right of the settings panel. It is not beside the box you just edited, and nothing takes effect until you click it. The box turns yellow when you edit it and stays yellow after saving, which means "set for this org unit" rather than "unsaved" — to confirm, reload the page and check your text is still there.
  7. Delivery takes a minute or two. On a staff browser, open chrome://policy and click Reload policies to confirm the settings arrived.

Only Workspace administrators can reach that console. That is Google's own access control, and it is why the organization palette is set there rather than inside the extension.
